What drives explosive moves at this price

Dated catalyst

FDA decision, results, investor day. INO is the case study: FDA approval on 30 10 2026, roughly 3× in a day, then it cooled.

Low float after a reverse split

A 1-for-30 split can leave about 1M shares in circulation, so any news moves the price violently — and fresh share sales usually follow.

Crowded short

High short interest on a small float. Good news forces shorts to buy back: the squeeze.

The traps

The red “filing” badges below link to recent SEC filings (S-1, S-3, F-1, 424B…) that usually mean new shares are coming.

If you trade any of these

  • Use limit orders only. Spreads under $1 can be 5–10%.
  • Size each position as money you'd accept losing entirely. A common rule is no more than 1–2% of the portfolio per name.
  • Click the red filing badges before buying: a fresh 424B or S-1 means shares are about to be sold.
  • Around reverse splits, trading may pause on the effective date.
